Random-state ab-initio tight binding

Electronic observables at million-atom scale.

RSATB turns large localized-orbital Hamiltonians — from DFT, DFTB, Slater-Koster or machine-learned models — into densities of states, real-space maps, conductivities and diffusion, without a single dense diagonalization. Non-orthogonal bases are native.
