Stochastic electronic-structure solver

Electronic structure
of large systems,
from random states.

RSATB computes densities of states, conductivities, optical spectra and real-space electron densities from tight-binding and DFT Hamiltonians, using stochastic trace estimation and Chebyshev expansions. It runs serially or with MPI and OpenMP across cluster nodes.

$ rsatb check run.json

Method

Spectra without diagonalization

The density of states, conductivities and optical spectra are obtained from Chebyshev moments of a few random vectors. The cost grows linearly with the number of atoms.

Graphene, nearest-neighbour pz model · 32,768 atoms · 8 random vectors · Tutorial 1

Architecture

Three numerical components,
one setup per input.

All tasks in an input share the atomic graph, the Hamiltonian, the overlap factor and the spectral window, which are built once.
